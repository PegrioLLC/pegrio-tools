Voice Styles

🌙 Low & Slow

Low baritone or deep alto. 85–95 wpm. Warm, close, near-whisper. Best for bedtime stories & deep relaxation.

☁️ Soft & Breath

Mid-range soft female. 90–100 wpm. Gentle, airy, unhurried. Best for guided sleep meditations & breathwork.

📖 Neutral Storyteller

Steady, even, no emotional peaks. 100–110 wpm. Best for fictional sleep stories ("nothing much happens" style).

🎧 ASMR-Adjacent

Close-mic, intimate, soft consonants. 80–90 wpm. Best for anxiety-driven insomnia.

FAQ
Can you write the script for me?

Yes — send me your topic and desired length, and I'll write a meditation script tailored to your purpose before recording.

What voice styles do you offer?

Soft female, deep calm male, warm neutral, and ASMR-adjacent. Send me a reference if you have one and I'll match it.

Do you add background music or ambience?

Yes — rain, forest, ocean, or ambient drones are included in Standard and Premium packages. Just describe the mood you want.

Why is the pacing slower than normal speech?

Meditation voiceover should be 90–110 words/min — about 30% slower than conversational speech. That single difference is what makes a voice calming rather than energizing.

What's your turnaround?

24–48 hours for most orders. Longer Premium packages (up to 30 min) take up to 3 days.
